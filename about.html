Our Story

What is FoodieHub?

ℹ️ Demonstration Project: FoodieHub is an internship learning project — not a real restaurant or delivery service.

FoodieHub is a fully functional online food ordering web application developed as part of a virtual internship program. It demonstrates practical skills in modern frontend development, Firebase integration, user authentication, and real-world application design.

Built with HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, and Firebase, FoodieHub showcases how modern web technologies can be used to create a complete, production-quality application — from user registration and menu browsing to cart management, checkout, and order tracking.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To demonstrate the development of a complete, secure, and user-friendly online food ordering platform — showing how modern web technologies and cloud services can be combined to build real-world applications.

🔭

Our Vision

To create a learning reference that other developers can study — demonstrating Firebase Authentication, Firestore CRUD operations, responsive design principles, and security best practices in a single, cohesive project.

How the Ordering Process Works

1️⃣

Create Account

Register with your email and password. Firebase Authentication secures your account.

2️⃣

Browse & Filter

Explore our menu filtered by category, price range, and dietary preference.

3️⃣

Add to Cart

Select items, choose quantities, and manage your cart easily.

4️⃣

Checkout

Enter your delivery address and confirm. Prices are verified against our database.

5️⃣

Track Order

Monitor your order status from Pending through to Delivered in My Orders.
